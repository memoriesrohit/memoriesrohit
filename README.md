## 🚀 Projects

### 🌈 [ScreenGlow](https://github.com/memoriesrohit/screenglow)
Ambient LED lighting that follows the colours on your screen. A Python app samples the screen edges, smooths and gamma-corrects the colours, and drives a WS2812B strip through an ESP32 (WLED over Wi-Fi) or an Arduino (USB). It also has a preview mode that needs no hardware.
`Python` · `NumPy` · `ESP32` · `Arduino` · `WS2812B`

### 🧍 [Avatar Studio](https://github.com/memoriesrohit/avatar-studio)
Realistic, rigged 3D people in the browser. You pick a person and an outfit (office formal, smart casual or casual), recolour it, set a facial expression and play moves like walk, wave, thumbs up and celebrate. You can export them as a GLB with a Mixamo-compatible skeleton, animations and face shapes, for Blender, Unity or Unreal. The characters are built in Blender with MakeHuman (MPFB) from CC0 assets.
`JavaScript` · `Three.js` · `Blender` · `Python` · `glTF`
